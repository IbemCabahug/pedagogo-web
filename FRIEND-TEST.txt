WHAT IT IS
----------
Pedagogo Desk is a free study tool that runs in your browser.
You give it a subject PDF (a chapter, a handout, a reviewer)
and it turns it into a calm study session: a quick summary,
key terms, little practice checks, and a review scheduler.

THE RULES (so the test is honest)
---------------------------------
1. Use your own LAPTOP/PC (not phone - this tool is for PC).
2. Use a subject PDF you already have for school (15-25 pages
   is ideal). Nothing confidential please - your PDF goes to
   Google's AI for one reading step.
3. Try everything ALONE first. If you are stuck for more than
   a minute, that is a finding! Write it down and move on.
4. There are no wrong answers. Confusion = useful data. :)

BEFORE YOU START (2 minutes)
----------------------------
[ ] Open this link in Chrome or Edge:
    https://pedagogo-dashboard.vercel.app
[ ] If it asks for an AI key: message me, I will send you an
    invite key to paste in.
[ ] Have your PDF ready where you can find it.

PART 1 - FIRST TOUCH (on purpose, no instructions!)
---------------------------------------------------
[ ] Open the link. Just look around for a moment.
[ ] Where would you click to add your PDF? (Do it.)

PART 2 - GENERATE (about 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------
[ ] Upload your PDF.
[ ] A question will ask what kind of studying you want.
    Pick whichever makes sense to you (remember which one
    you picked and why - I will ask).
[ ] Wait for the result sheet. While waiting: did the wait
    feel okay? Too long? Confusing?

PART 3 - READ LIKE A STUDENT (about 5 minutes)
----------------------------------------------
[ ] Find the short summary (TL;DR). Is it actually short?
[ ] Find the key terms list. Try the little test buttons.
[ ] Somewhere there is a banner about checking the ORIGINAL
    pages. Open at least one page citation - did it jump to
    the right place?
[ ] Find the card that tells you what to study next.
    Follow what it says.

PART 4 - PRACTICE + OFFLINE (about 3 minutes)
---------------------------------------------
[ ] Push the practice items to the reviewer (it will ask).
[ ] Try one flashcard / question there.
[ ] Try to INSTALL it as an app, if you can find how.
[ ] Turn OFF your wifi, then reload the page.
    Does it still open? Then turn wifi back on.
