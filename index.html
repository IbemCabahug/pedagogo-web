III. Procedure: The 4As Framework
1. Activity (Engage & Hook)

Games, picture prompts, diagnostic hooks, or pair activities to pique learner curiosity.

2. Analysis (Guiding Critical Questions)

Questions that guide learners to discover principles and analyze clues from the activity.

3. Abstraction (Core Concept & Generalization)

Structured pedagogical delivery of definitions, rules, and fundamental insights.

4. Application (Hands-on Practice)

Differentiated tasks, group scenarios, or authentic creative practice.

III. Procedure: The 7Es Constructivist Framework
1. Elicit (Access Prior Knowledge)

Determine what students already know about the topic through quick diagnostic questions.

2. Engage (Captivate Curiosity)

Hook the learners' attention with an intriguing scenario, video clip, or puzzle.

3. Explore (Hands-on Investigation)

Learners manipulate materials, test ideas, and investigate patterns collaboratively.

4. Explain (Clarify & Define Concepts)

Students describe their findings; teacher clarifies official definitions and rules.

5. Elaborate (Extend Concept to New Situations)

Deepen understanding by applying learned concepts in more complex, authentic settings.

6. Evaluate (Formative Assessment)

Assess learner grasp and self-reflection against initial learning objectives.

7. Extend (Real-World Enrichment)

Challenge learners to connect the concept to daily life, family, or personal reading habits.
